Certifications and codes

What certifications do I get?

One DEKA coaching code and one SGX code. Each can be used twice, so up to two people per code.

Can I give a code to my staff?

Yes. Both are transferable. If you're already SGX certified, pass that code to a coach or staff member.

How do I use a code?

Apply it at checkout when registering for the course.

My code failed, or access never arrived.

Email affiliatesupport@spartan.com and CC me. Include a screenshot of the error and the code you used, and I'll chase it down on my end.

Getting your event on the calendar

How do I request an event?

Submit the Affiliate Service Form, found in the Affiliate Toolkit.

How far in advance?

Eight weeks minimum. Twelve weeks if you want full promotional support. Events launch sequentially by date, so earlier requests get more runway.

There's a second reason to book early. DEKA runs email campaigns to athletes within a 200 mile radius of every DEKA FIT event, spotlighting affiliate STRONG and MILE events as training grounds. Only events already live on the calendar get included. The 2026 DEKA FIT calendar includes Boston in June, Pennsylvania in July, and New York in September.

How many hours should I request?

Four to five for your first event. Limited spots create scarcity, and a tighter schedule is easier to run. If you sell out, submit another request to add heats.

How many athletes fit in a heat?

A heat is a 30-minute block. Per lane, four athletes for STRONG or ATLAS, three for MILE. The run leg takes longer, which is why MILE takes three instead of four.

Multiply by your lanes. Two STRONG lanes means eight athletes per 30-minute heat.

We set final capacity based on how many lanes you plan to have. You'll see it reflected on the launch email you receive once your event goes live.

How much space do I need?

STRONG: 1,800 sq ft for two lanes. One lane works in 1,200 sq ft if you have 40 feet of length.

MILE: 1,800 sq ft plus 80m of run space. The mile can run as an 80m out and back, or a 160m loop.

Zones 6 and 9 are set up as 10m down and back.

Event day

How is the day structured?

Formats run sequentially, not at the same time. Thirty-minute blocks, back to back. Age group heats first, then team heats within each format.

A typical six-hour day: STRONG 8:00 to 10:30, MILE 10:30 to 1:00, ATLAS 1:00 to 2:00.

Is there a time cap?

ATLAS runs 30 minutes per athlete. That's the duration, not a cutoff.

STRONG and MILE have no cap. Athletes finish when they finish, and the 30-minute block is how heats are scheduled.

Where do I find heat times?

Log into TicketSocket using the credentials in your Event Launch email. From the dashboard, choose Export Tickets from the Export Results dropdown. Wave times are in column AM. Open the file in Excel rather than Google Sheets so the formatting stays intact.

When should athletes arrive?

No later than 30 minutes before their start time, with photo ID and their confirmation email. They sign a digital waiver at check-in.

How many volunteers do I need?

Three judges per lane, plus two at check-in handling time submissions, calculating DEKA Marks, and handing out shirts and medals.

What supplies should I have?

Pens, sharpies, wristbands for heat assignments, measuring tape (a measuring wheel is required for official MILE measurement), and sidewalk chalk, cones, or tape for Zones 6 and 9.

How do I time it?

Two devices, a phone and a stopwatch. A running clock works well as backup. Start athletes on the whole minute so the subtraction math stays easy.

Running the event: check-in, scoring, results

What do I need to print beforehand?

Two things:

Scorecards. Athletes fill one out right after check-in and hand it to their judge before starting.

Paper waivers. Athletes sign the online waiver at check-in, but print at least 20 paper copies as backup in case of technical trouble.

How does check-in work?

Through the DEKA check-in kiosk. The link and instructions come in your event materials.

Can I take walk-up registrations on the day?

Yes, as long as your event hasn't sold out.

Credit or debit card only. Cash is not accepted, with no exceptions.

Everyone must go through the official kiosk. DEKA Marks submitted for athletes who weren't registered through it may be deleted and excluded from the global leaderboard.

The system needs five to ten minutes to sync same-day registrations before those athletes appear for scoring.

How do I enter results?

Select the athlete and check every detail: product (STRONG, MILE, or ATLAS), first and last name, gender, and date of birth. Enter their DEKA Mark in the Mark field using mm:ss only. Click Finish to save.

What happens to the scorecards?

Athletes can photograph theirs, but your gym keeps the physical copy. It's what resolves any DEKA Mark question later.

Where do results show up?

Your gym gets a live leaderboard you can watch during the event. The link is sent the week of your event.

Afterward, your results live in the Past Events section:

Leaderboards

You're CC'd on every Certificate of Completion email, so you can forward one if an athlete mistyped their address at registration.

An athlete's name or details are wrong on the leaderboard.

Submit an Affiliate Service Form with the event date and type, plus the athlete's full name, gender, date of birth, email, and DEKA Mark. For teams, include both participants.

What should my judges review beforehand?

The DEKA Official Rulebook and the movement standards video, both in the Toolkit. Send them to every judge before event day. Judges who know the standards keep the day moving and the results clean.

Something's wrong with my registration list.

Report it by Wednesday at 12:00 PM EST the week of your event, so there's time to fix it. For anything that comes up during the event itself, email affiliatesupport@spartan.com. Weekend response times run longer.

Collateral

When does it arrive?

Typically two weeks before your event, sometimes as late as four days out.

What should I do when it arrives?

Open the boxes and count shirts and medals against your registrations the day they land, not the day before the event. Short or incorrect orders can usually be fixed with enough notice.

How are shirt sizes decided?

By what each participant selected during registration. Inventory is based on general demand though, so exact sizes aren't guaranteed. If someone's size isn't in the box, give them the closest available so they leave with a finisher shirt.

I have leftover collateral.

Hold it for your next event. If you need to return anything, or want to clear out at year end, submit the Affiliate Service Form for a prepaid return label. Returning promptly matters, since unused stock is what keeps other affiliates' events supplied.

Will I still get pre-event packs?

Yes. Holding leftovers doesn't affect future shipments.

Revenue share and payouts

How does the split work?

You keep 60% of net event revenue as the hosting facility.

What can I actually expect to make?

A worked example: 60 participants at $60 is $3,600 in registrations. Your 60% is $2,160.

You set your own price. Most affiliates charge $45 to $60 for STRONG and $55 to $65 for MILE. Many run more than 60 participants. Adding team registration raises it further.

Beyond the event itself, affiliates generate revenue from DEKA-specific training programs (a 4 to 8 week block with 20 participants at $40 is $800, run quarterly that's $3,200) and from new memberships that come out of event leads. One to three new members per event at a $40 membership fee is roughly $1,440 a year.

Across all three, the annual opportunity runs around $10,000.

Who do I ask about payments?

Send all payout, revenue share, and 1099 questions to affiliatesupport@spartan.com and CC me. I don't process payments, so going direct gets you a faster answer.

Event types

DEKA STRONG
10 zones, no running.
DEKA MILE
10 zones with 0.1 miles between each. One mile total.
DEKA ATLAS
10 zones, 30 minutes per athlete.
DEKA FIT
10 zones with 500m between each, 5K total. Run by DEKA HQ, not hosted by affiliates.
DEKA Teams
teams of 2 split the work. Available for STRONG, MILE, and FIT. Good for first-timers and for elite athletes chasing speed.
DEKA Youth
age categories 10-11 and 12-13, male and female. Age is determined by their age on event day.
DEKA Trifecta
completing a STRONG, a MILE, and a FIT in the same calendar year. Athletes collect their hexes. In 2026 there are two medal and shirt designs, one for each half of the year, so athletes who earn it twice get both.

Zone modifications for STRONG and MILE

Two zones change from the FIT standard:

Zone 4
Med Ball Sit-up Throws become Med Ball Sit-ups. The ball must touch the ground behind the head at the bottom and between the legs at the top.
Zone 8
Dead Ball Wall Overs become Dead Ball Shoulder Overs. Full hip and knee extension, weight over the top of the shoulder.

Send both to your judges before event day.

Programming between events

What is the DEKA Training System?

Three weekly workouts built on three pillars, all using the 10 zones:

DEKA FORGE strength and power. 4 rounds, 30 seconds on, 30 seconds off.

DEKA BURN anaerobic conditioning. 3 rounds, 60 seconds on, 30 seconds off.

DEKA GRIT endurance. 2 rounds, 90 seconds on, 30 seconds off.

It's in the Toolkit and it's the simplest way to get members ready for an event.

What does the member journey look like?

The affiliates who get the most out of DEKA run this loop:

  1. Establish a member's DEKA Mark
  2. Build a training plan around it
  3. Re-test every 30 to 45 days
  4. Run an in-house STRONG or MILE event quarterly
  5. Send members to a DEKA FIT event annually
  6. Repeat

A Mark that members want to beat is what turns a one-time event into a training program.

What are the quarterly challenges?

Team workouts DEKA runs each quarter with gamified leaderboards. They're easy to execute, they give non-members a low-pressure first taste of DEKA, and they bring new people through your door. Details come out ahead of each quarter.
